espc-model-repo

One model, published on its own: the US Navy ESPC-D-V02 surface and 50 m currents, and its sea surface temperature and salinity, for the C4PO ocean map. The Navy sea ice comes off the same model and is published by realtime-data-repo instead — deliberately, so that moving one product between repositories stays a thing this arrangement can do rather than a thing it once did.

It is not a public API. The files under map/ exist to be read by the map, and their layout is that map's contract.

What is fresh, what is held, and which roots this origin serves: status/status.json. The run's intent and outcome sit beside it as plan.json and receipt.json.

How it works, and why it holds no code of its own: the repository README.
